Three dots, three meanings

The two numbers are the metres between your remote and the buggy, in Follow-Me, auto-return and return-to-me alike.

One dot hopping
waiting — armed, not following yet

Two steady dots
following you

Filling down from the top
the buggy is coming to you

Manual always winsIf GPS, compass or any automatic mode fails, the automation switches itself off and you can drive home by hand.
“St” means not workingOne signal for everything that stopped or was refused, always with the long buzz when you did not cause it. No new words to learn.
The screen tells the truthA mode is shown as ON only when the buggy confirms it. “Waiting” is only shown when a squeeze will really bring the buggy back.
Glance quiz

Can you read it in a third of a second?

The screen keeps running under a cover. Each round the cover opens for the time you pick, like glancing down at your hand while riding, then you tap what you saw. 12 rounds, 3 each of off, waiting, following and returning. Start at 5 s to learn the looks, then go down to 0.33 s and turn on Bob and Wet.
